HERITAGE TIMELINE

Five Generations. One Silhouette.

Hover or tap any generation to smoothly slide the car aside and reveal its engineering milestones.

GEN 01 · 1997–1998
1997 – 1998

993 Carrera S

The Final Air-Cooled Icon
3.6L NA Air-Cooled · 285 PS · 340 Nm · 0–100 in 5.3s · 273 km/h

Produced exclusively in 1997–1998, the 993 Carrera S represents the ultimate and final expression of the air-cooled 911. Powered by a 3.6-litre naturally aspirated flat-six paired with a 6-speed manual or 4-speed Tiptronic S in rear-wheel drive, it stands as the most prized generation among purists worldwide.

GEN 02 · 1999–2005
1999 – 2005

996 Carrera 4S

The Water-Cooled Revolution
3.6L Water-Cooled · 320 PS · 380 Nm · 0–100 in 5.0s · 280 km/h

The 996 marked Porsche’s historic transition into water-cooled flat-six architecture. Carrying the revered Carrera 4S moniker, the MkII featured an all-wheel-drive powertrain, wide Turbo body shell, and 320 PS, rewriting high-speed stability benchmarks despite its controversial "fried egg" headlights.

GEN 03 · 2005–2012
2005 – 2012

997 Carrera S

Classic Proportions & PDK Debut
3.8L DFI Flat-Six · 385 PS · 420 Nm · 0–100 in 4.3s · 301 km/h

Iconic round headlamps returned with the 997. The MkII (997.2) introduced Direct Fuel Injection (DFI) and the world-first 7-speed PDK dual-clutch transmission, enabling rapid gear switches, sub-4.3s sprint times, and legendary reliability that cemented modern Carrera S pedigree.

GEN 04 · 2012–2019
2012 – 2019

991 Carrera S

Final NA to Twin-Turbo Era
3.0L Twin-Turbo · 450 PS · 530 Nm · 0–100 in 3.7s · 308 km/h

A pivotal transition. The 991.1 was the final naturally aspirated Carrera S (3.8L, 400 PS). The 991.2 ushered in standard twin-turbocharging, surging torque from 440 to 530 Nm. Its wider track, electric steering precision, and rear-axle steering created a benchmark everyday supercar.

GEN 05 · 2019–PRESENT
2019 – Present

992 Carrera S

The Most Powerful Carrera S Ever
3.0L Twin-Turbo (992.2) · 480 PS · 530 Nm · 0–100 in 3.3s · 308 km/h

The pinnacle of 60 years of development. The current 992.2 delivers 480 PS (473 bhp) exclusively through an 8-speed PDK transmission with BorgWarner turbos and top-mounted intercooler. Equipped with PASM, PTV Plus, and 408mm GTS front brakes, it is the most refined and rapid Carrera S in history.

Mechanical Anatomy

01 / INDUCTION

Twin BorgWarner Turbos

Upgraded BorgWarner turbochargers and Turbo-derived top-mounted intercooler generating +30 hp over 992.1.

02 / COMBUSTION

2,981 cc Boxer Architecture

91.0 × 76.4 mm bore-and-stroke, 24-valve DOHC head with high-precision direct fuel injection.

03 / LUBRICATION

Integrated Dry-Sump

Multi-stage oil scavenger pumps maintain flawless oil pressure through high lateral G-forces up to 7,500 rpm redline.

04 / DRIVETRAIN

8-Speed PDK Dual-Clutch

Millisecond gear swaps driving the rear wheels. Integrated electronic limited-slip differential with PTV Plus.

05 / THERMODYNAMICS

10.2:1 Compression

Wide 2,200–6,000 rpm torque plateau generating 530 Nm for immediate throttle response.

06 / SPECIFIC OUTPUT

157 HP Per Litre

480 PS (473 hp) yielding a remarkable power-to-weight ratio of 3.28 kg/hp in a 1,553 kg package.
